is only limited by the number of your ideas.

but without knowledge of programming they are almost impossible.

---

dog and pony of High School must teach programming.

Because in the age of thinking machines,
inability to program is a real disability.

as a real cruelty, that sentences brilliant minds to poverty.

---

and more violations, that is not sustainable.

The future will demand what every human being is capable of,
unique, exceptional abilities.

Brought on the only way that excellence can be reached,
by years, or even decades of authentic and meaningful education.

---

just like each programming class enables with new programming powers.

as the next gateway in life is a betrayal and a crime.

that contradict the promise of growing up and learning .
