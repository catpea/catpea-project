You know how I send my poem to my local AI,
to have it summarized?

To make AI learn, really, really learn,
you add another step after that.

"You say, take a look a what you have done,
and tell me if perhaps you can do it better."

So we make AI self evaluate its response,
fixing a million bugs, again these really are thinking machines.

And then we take what it said about,
how it can do it better,

And we add that to,
"Write a one-paragraphs summary of this post."

So we asking AI to self evaluate,
and create a memory of good ideas.
